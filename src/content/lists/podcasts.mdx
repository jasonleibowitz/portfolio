# Intro

I listen to _a lot_ of podcasts. In fact, I'm subscribed to over 50 different podcasts in my app of choice, [Pocket Casts](https://pocketcasts.com/). I'm often asked for podcast recommendations. This list is my absolute favorite podcasts organized by category.

Please [reach out](https://twitter.com/jasonleibowitz) if you agree or disagree with any of these, and especially if you have any recommendations for new podcasts for me to check out.

## News

- [Up First](https://www.npr.org/podcasts/510318/up-first) - One of the two podcasts I listen to every morning. Episodes are short, around 10-15 minutes, and go over all the important news items of the day.
- [The Daily](https://www.nytimes.com/column/the-daily)
- [The Indicator from Planet Money](https://www.npr.org/podcasts/510325/the-indicator-from-planet-money)
- [Planet Money](https://www.npr.org/sections/money/)
