## Design Specificity Verdict

The visual system is specific; the product design is not. Orbit is a genuinely authored world (aurora on warm paper, one spectrum held with discipline, mono-means-data enforced, orbit ring). The composition underneath is a stock 2023 portfolio template, and the rotating hero verb occupies the most valuable real estate while asserting something true of every engineer who has shipped. The four PRODUCT.md positioning claims appear nowhere in the hero. Deeper issue: designed for a design system, not for a job search — nothing in the layout knows the primary visitor is a skeptical hiring manager on a phone with eight seconds.

## What's Working

1. One-spectrum discipline actually held — violet→cyan marks active state and nothing else, so "what is active" is never ambiguous in either theme.
2. Mono-means-data enforced — every date, count, tag, stack item is JetBrains Mono; no prose ever is. Why metadata-dense pages don't feel cluttered.
3. Layout integrity survives measurement — zero horizontal overflow across 6 pages x 6 widths x 2 themes; reduced-motion honored in three modules; 9/9 images with correct alt.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Header wordmark invisible — 1.00:1 contrast, both themes
Header.astro:23 uses `text-base` for font size, but `--color-base` in global.css:20 generates a `text-base` color utility that shadows it. Verified: rgb(252,250,246) on rgb(252,250,246). Font-size also never applies (renders 17px). BaseLayout.astro:73 same bug on skip link, accidentally legible on bg-ink.
Fix: `text-[1rem] text-ink` on both; document the `--color-*` namespace collision.

### [P0] H1 reads "I build things that shiplastdelightscale."
Cycler.astro renders all four words at opacity:0 with no aria-hidden. Opacity-0 stays in the a11y tree. Verified via textContent; no aria-label present. Screen readers announce nonsense; Google indexes nonsense.
Fix: aria-hidden on the word spans, aria-label on the h1.

### [P0] No contact affordance on six of eight routes
Footer renders "Let's build something." as a <p> with nothing to click. Verified: zero mailto, zero résumé links in the footer. Conversion surface is two buttons on two routes, against success metric #1 and Product Principle #5.
Fix: real Email me + Download résumé in the footer on every page. Also the correct home for the two-phase swappable availability value.

### [P1] Developer scaffolding shipping to visitors
PhNote is documented as "a note to Jason" and renders in production naming a source file. placeholder-copy is a bare dotted underline with no title, aria-description, or legend — so a sighted visitor misreads it and a screen-reader user gets nothing and takes placeholder text as fact, the exact failure the policy exists to prevent.

### [P1] Device frames read as a loading state
Gray slabs in a rounded outline are the universal skeleton idiom. They carry 30–40% of visual weight on / and /projects, converting the strongest positioning evidence into apparent breakage.
