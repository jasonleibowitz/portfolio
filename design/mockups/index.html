What changed

  • Section spacing. One --section token — clamp(44px, 24px + 2.6vw, 72px) — used by every block, with adjacent sections no longer doubling their padding and a fixed 24px heading→content gap. The v1 gaps were about 40% larger and drifted from block to block.
  • Headline word cycles. ship → last → delight → scale, 2.8s each, cross-fading with a blur and a small rise. The container animates its width so the full stop doesn't jump. Frozen on the first word under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Theme toggle. Sun morphs to moon, and the change itself sweeps out as a circle from the button (View Transitions API). Browsers without it swap instantly.
  • JL monogram replaces the anonymous dot. Gradient squircle, J and L sharing a baseline — works as a favicon at 32px.
  • Experience gets three placeholder bullets per role, a pulse travelling down the spine, and a toggle so you can compare even spacing against duration-proportional spacing.
  • Writing index is one list with no year headings — date, read time and tags on every row — plus a sticky tag rail with counts that filters in place. On mobile the rail becomes a scrollable chip row.
  • Post measure 40rem → 46rem, body type up to 22px, tags promoted to chips under the title, and a reading-progress bar under the header in the headline gradient.
  • Footnotes open in a popover where you are; the ↩ in the footnote list scrolls back to the reference and opens that popover.
  • About has a photo grid — three of your real photos are in there now.
  • Dark mode lifted off near-black: base #0B0B12 → #16161F, text #ECEAF5 → #F4F2FB, violet and cyan both lightened.
  • Blur count 12 → 2. Only the header and dock sit over scrolling content, so only they keep a live backdrop-filter; panels over the static aurora use a flat translucent fill.

Four judgment calls I made — say the word and I'll flip any of them.

  • Projects stayed on the homepage, but as two large cards with device shots instead of small ones. The apps are your strongest asset and someone who never leaves the homepage should still see them.
  • Writing stayed too, with the blurb you suggested, three latest posts, and the inline link in the hero paragraph as well — so you get both options you floated, and can cut either.
  • Photos are a grid, not a carousel. Everything visible at once, no JS, nothing hidden from search engines. A carousel starts earning its keep above about eight photos.
  • Duration-proportional timeline is built but off by default — the toggle next to "Experience" switches it. It's honest, but a short role gets squeezed and a long one leaves a hole. Worth looking at before deciding.
